## Youth Training

We teach kids badminton. Our coaches go into schools in Linz and run badminton sessions during PE and after-school care. Kids who want more join our club training and compete in tournaments.

**Tuesday** 5:00 &ndash; 7:00 PM
AHS Solar City, Heliosallee 140&ndash;142

**Friday** 6:00 &ndash; 7:30 PM
Europagymnasium Auhof, Aubrunnerweg 4

Your child can just come along and try it. Indoor shoes are enough, we have rackets.

## Adult Training

## Membership

Annual fees (from September 2025):

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Adults | 154 &euro; |
| Students (up to 26) | 84 &euro; |
| Youth (15&ndash;18) | 55 &euro; |
| Children (up to 14) | 44 &euro; |
| Family card (2 adults + 2 children) | 264 &euro; |
| Kids training per semester | 38.50 &euro; |
| Supporting members | 44 &euro; |

For league matches you also need a player licence from the Austrian Badminton Federation.

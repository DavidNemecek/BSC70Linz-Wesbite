# Privacy Policy

## 1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:

BSC 70 Linz  
Reuchlinstraße 4  
4020 Linz  
E-mail: kontakt@bsc70linz.at

## 2. Which data do we collect?

If you contact us via a contact form or by e-mail, we process the data you provide, in particular:

- Name
- E-mail address
- Message/content of your request

## 3. Purpose of processing

We process this data solely to handle and reply to your request.

## 4. Legal basis

The processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures or performance of a contract), insofar as your request is aimed at this.

## 5. Storage period

We store your data only for as long as necessary to process the request, but for no longer than 3 years unless longer statutory retention obligations apply.

## 6. No disclosure to third parties

We do not disclose your data to third parties unless we are legally required to do so.

## 7. No automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

## 8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

- access
- rectification
- erasure
- restriction of processing
- object to processing

## 9. Right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
